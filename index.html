A small tool that turns your notes into flashcards. You paste in a block of text and it pulls out the key terms and definitions.

I built it because making flashcards by hand took longer than actually revising them.

  • Export to a CSV so you can import it into other apps.
  • Shuffle mode so you cant just memorise the order not the answers
